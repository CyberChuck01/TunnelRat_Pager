TUNNEL R.A.T. // T‑Pager

Browser installer for the LILYGO T‑LoRa Pager (ESP32‑S3)

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop (Web Serial required — mobile and Safari won't work).
  2. Connect the T‑Pager over USB‑C and power it on.
  3. Click Install, pick the serial port, and let it flash. Choose "erase device" for a clean install.
Your browser can't flash here — open this page in desktop Chrome or Edge.
Nothing is uploaded anywhere — flashing happens locally, browser → USB.
